# TAWAR demo — VO script (Liam, ElevenLabs)

This agent haggles for you. It is not allowed to overspend.
You give Tawar three things: an item, a target price, and a hard ceiling. Then it negotiates with the seller, round by round, on its own.
The ceiling is a leash, and code holds it. The mandate is signed. Offers above the ceiling are clamped, deals above it are refused, and a kill switch can stop the session between rounds.
Before the first offer, Tawar writes a strategy plan. Every move after that carries its reasoning in the open, so you can audit why it held, conceded, or walked away.
When a seller drops too far in one step, the engine caps that too. Enforcement is not a suggestion. The code decides.
This real session ran eight rounds and closed at three hundred ninety dollars, sixty dollars under the list price. Every deal in the ledger sits under its ceiling.
Tawar also negotiates in Bahasa Indonesia, in rupiah, for its home market.
Built with SERV Reasoning, for the Kite track.
