# TAWAR — X launch post (draft)

## Main post

I gave an AI agent one hard rule: haggle for me, but never pay above my ceiling.

It haggled a $450 iPhone for 8 rounds and closed at $390, saving $60. Against a seller who would not budge, it held its price for 8 rounds and walked away with nothing rather than break the limit.

The mandate is signed before round 1. The transcript is hash-chained. The ceiling is enforced by code, not by the model's good intentions. This is Tawar, my build for the @openservai SERV Hackathon 02, on the @GoKiteAI track.

Demo and full transcripts in the replies.

## Reply post (savings ledger variant)

Every Tawar session lands in a public savings ledger, wins and walk-aways alike.

So far: 12 sessions, 4 closed deals. $135 saved across the USD deals, Rp1.450.000 saved across the IDR deals, including Rp850.000 in Local Market Mode negotiating in Bahasa Indonesia. The other eight sessions closed nothing, because nothing on offer fit inside the mandate.

An agent you can audit, with a limit you can verify. That is the whole point.
